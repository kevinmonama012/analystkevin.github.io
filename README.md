### 🧩 About Me

I'm a **full stack data analyst** — I work across the entire data lifecycle:
sourcing and cleaning raw data, building statistical and machine learning
models, engineering pipelines, and shipping the results as dashboards, APIs,
and reports that people actually use. My focus is **South African economic
and business data** — turning public statistics into clear, decision-ready
analysis.

- 🔭 Currently building a portfolio of end-to-end SA economic data projects
- 🌱 Deepening skills in time-series forecasting & data engineering pipelines
- 💬 Ask me about: regression modelling, index numbers (CPI/GDP), Python data pipelines, dashboarding
- 📫 Reach me: **katlegokevinmonama@gmail.com** · **kevinmonama012@gmail.com**
